Executive Scientific Summary (BLUF)

Auditory distress, otalgia-associated tension, and subjective tinnitus arise from maladaptive neuroplastic hyperactivity within tonotopic areas of the primary auditory cortex (A1). AuralRelief (myearshurt.me) operationalizes three distinct audiological mechanisms: (1) Tailored Notched Sound Therapy (TNST) with a real-time BiquadFilter (Q=5.0) inducing lateral cortical inhibition; (2) 1/f² Brownian acoustic masking providing dense sub-bass coverage (20–250 Hz) at a -6 dB/octave roll-off to eliminate high-frequency acoustic irritation; and (3) dual-carrier 6Hz theta and 40Hz gamma entrainment to facilitate parasympathetic down-regulation during clinical transit and waiting room periods.

Core Academic Literature & Clinical Studies

1. Tailor-Made Notched Music Training (TMNMT) in Chronic Tinnitus

PNAS (2010)

Okamoto, H., Stracke, H., Stoll, W., & Pantev, C. | Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 107(3), 1207–1210.

Key Finding: In a randomized controlled study, patients exposed to custom-notched audio matching their tinnitus frequency showed significant reductions in magnetoencephalographic (MEG) auditory cortex evoked responses (N1m) and perceived loudness compared to controls, proving lateral inhibition reduces neural hyperactivity.

DOI: 10.1073/pnas.0911268107

2. Lateral Inhibition in Auditory Cortex Reshapes Maladaptive Tonotopy

Hearing Research (2012)

Pantev, C., Okamoto, H., & Teismann, H. | Hearing Research, 295, 157–163.

Key Finding: Notched sound therapy exerts targeted lateral cortical inhibition that suppresses the edge-frequency synchrony typical of tinnitus, providing an empirical basis for browser-based real-time notch DSP implementations.

DOI: 10.1016/j.heares.2012.03.003

3. Neurophysiological Model of Tinnitus & Habituation (TRT)

Neuroscience Research (1990)

Jastreboff, P. J. | Neuroscience Research, 8(4), 221–254.

Key Finding: Established that auditory habituation requires the presence of low-level broadband background noise to decrease the signal-to-noise ratio of phantom auditory perception, demonstrating why dead silence worsens tinnitus and ear distress.

PMID: 2174124
