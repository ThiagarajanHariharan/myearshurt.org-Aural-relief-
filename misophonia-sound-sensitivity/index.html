Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best noise color for misophonia?

Brown noise and green noise are generally preferred over white noise for misophonia because they have soft, low-frequency dominance that buffers sharp clicking and chewing noises without sounding harsh.

Is misophonia a problem with the ears or the brain?

Misophonia is a neuro-auditory condition involving hyper-connectivity between the auditory cortex and the limbic/salience network (anterior insular cortex), not a physical ear impairment.
