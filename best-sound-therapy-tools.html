Detailed In-Depth Analysis

1. AuralRelief (myearshurt.me) — Best Overall Free Acoustic Tool

Our Top Recommendation

Pros: 100% free with zero advertisements or subscription paywalls. Runs directly in any web browser on iPhone, Android, or desktop with instant start. Features an interactive Biquad notch filter (125 Hz–16 kHz), 3D spatial audio orbit, 4-stem mixer, and dedicated evidence-based guides for ear pain, waiting rooms, and nighttime sleep.

Cons: Focuses on acoustic comfort and sound therapy rather than 1-on-1 audiology consultation or cognitive behavioral therapy.

2. Endel — Best for General Circadian Focus & Ambient Music

Pros: Stunning visual design, adaptive biofeedback (Apple Watch HRV sync), and ambient generative soundscapes by popular artists (Grimes, James Blake).

Cons: $89.99/year subscription. Lacks clinical condition targeting (ear pain, ear fullness, hyperacusis) and does not provide customizable notch filters for specific ringing frequencies.

3. Oto Tinnitus — Best for Structured CBT Programs

Pros: Strong clinical foundation with university-led randomized controlled trials (RCTs). Combines sound masking with structured cognitive behavioral therapy modules for chronic tinnitus habituation.

Cons: High cost (often sold as part of clinical packages exceeding $500). Requires downloading an app and creating an account before access.

4. ReSound Relief — Best for Multi-Soundscape Stem Layering

Pros: Developed by hearing aid manufacturer GN Hearing. Allows users to combine up to 5 background sound layers (ocean, wind, gentle music) into a personalized mix.

Cons: Interface feels dated compared to modern web apps. Primarily functions as a lead-generation funnel for ReSound hearing aids.
