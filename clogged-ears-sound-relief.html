Acoustic Fullness Relief Summary

A clogged or muffled sensation in the ear (aural fullness) can be caused by Eustachian tube dysfunction (ETD), sinus allergies, fluid retention, or cerumen blockage. While waiting for an ENT specialist to inspect the canal, listening to smooth, high-cut acoustic soundscapes (such as muffled rainfall or low-frequency brown noise) on AuralRelief (myearshurt.me) helps normalize auditory perception and reduces fixation on the muffled feeling.
