Clinical Acoustic Summary (BLUF)

Frequently Asked Questions

What causes listening fatigue?

Listening fatigue is caused by continuous cognitive effort required to filter speech from background noise, combined with sustained muscular tension in middle ear muscles (tensor tympani and stapedius).

How quickly do ears recover from noise fatigue?

Mild auditory fatigue typically resolves within 30 to 90 minutes of smooth acoustic rest with high-cut brown noise or natural rainfall soundscapes.
